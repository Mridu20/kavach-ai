# Ref No: INSP-2026-PSU-8849

--- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY ---
Statutory NDT (Non-Destructive Testing) ultrasonic thickness measurement and visual inspection performed on Pressure Vessel V-102 and Hydrocarbon Feed Line 3.

--- INSPECTION FINDINGS ---

   - Status: DEFECT DETECTED
   - Finding: Surface cracking observed near heat-affected zone of weld joint B-12 during Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI).
   - Severity: HIGH
   - Recommended Action: Perform 100% Radiographic Testing (RT) prior to statutory clearance.

2. Component: Hydrocarbon Feed Line 3 (PIPE-SEC-03)
   - Status: CRITICAL WALL THINNING
   - Finding: Wall thickness measured 2.8mm against design minimum requirement of 3.2mm (corrosion allowance exceeded).
   - Severity: CRITICAL
   - Recommended Action: Immediate emergency derating or containment sleeve installation mandated as per OISD-STD-118 Section 4.2.1.

--- STATUTORY RECOMMENDATIONS ---
- Mandate Form-B statutory approval note signed by Chief Inspection Engineer prior to resuming hydrocarbon service.
- Re-verify wall thickness with UT probe after sleeve placement.
