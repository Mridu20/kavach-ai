MAINTENANCE MANUAL: HEAVY INDUSTRIAL STEAM TURBINE, MODEL T-850
Document ID: KAVACH-MM-TURBINE-2025
Revision: 1.2
Effective Date: 15 January 2025
Issuing Authority: Rotating Equipment Engineering
Classification: INTERNAL - CONFIDENTIAL

SECTION 1: VIBRATION MONITORING AND TRIP THRESHOLDS

1.1 Normal operating vibration for the T-850 turbine rotor is below 1.5 mm per
second RMS measured at the bearing housings in the horizontal plane.

1.2 An alarm must be raised at 2.8 mm per second RMS. The unit remains in
service but requires investigation within the same shift.

1.3 The critical trip threshold is 4.5 mm per second RMS. The machine
protection system will initiate an automatic trip at this level without
operator intervention.

1.4 Axial displacement of the rotor exceeding 0.5 mm from the certified
reference position requires immediate shutdown and inspection of the thrust
bearing assembly.

1.5 Vibration readings must be recorded at four-hour intervals during normal
operation and at 30 minute intervals during start-up and run-down.

SECTION 2: LUBRICATION SYSTEM

2.1 Approved lubricant is synthetic turbine oil grade ISO VG 46. Substitution
requires written approval from Rotating Equipment Engineering.

2.2 Oil filter elements must be changed every 2,000 operating hours, or
immediately when the differential pressure across the filter exceeds 1.2 bar,
whichever occurs first.

2.3 Lubricating oil temperature at the bearing inlet must be maintained between
40 and 50 degrees Celsius. Outlet temperature above 70 degrees Celsius
indicates bearing distress and requires investigation.

2.4 Oil analysis samples must be drawn quarterly and tested for viscosity,
water content, total acid number and particle count. Water content above 500
parts per million requires the charge to be replaced.

SECTION 3: SEALS AND CONTAINMENT

3.1 Secondary containment seal replacement is required every 12 months.

3.2 Labyrinth seal clearances must be measured at every major overhaul.
Clearance exceeding 0.8 mm requires seal strip replacement.

3.3 Gland steam condenser vacuum must be maintained between 0.85 and 0.95 bar
absolute to prevent steam leakage along the shaft.

SECTION 4: OVERHAUL SCHEDULE

4.1 Minor inspection: every 8,000 operating hours. Covers bearing inspection,
oil system service, control valve stroke testing and instrument calibration.

4.2 Major overhaul: every 32,000 operating hours or six calendar years,
whichever occurs first. Requires full rotor removal and blade inspection.

4.3 Blade tip erosion exceeding 1.5 mm in depth requires blade replacement.
Erosion between 0.8 mm and 1.5 mm may be dressed and returned to service with
engineering approval.

SECTION 5: START-UP PROCEDURE

5.1 Confirm turning gear has operated continuously for a minimum of four hours
prior to admitting steam.

5.2 Steam admission must follow the certified run-up curve. Maximum permitted
acceleration is 150 revolutions per minute per minute below 2,000 rpm.

5.3 Hold at 1,200 rpm for a minimum of 20 minutes for thermal soak before
continuing acceleration to rated speed.

5.4 Critical speed range is 2,100 to 2,600 rpm. The machine must traverse this
band without dwelling and must not be held within it under any circumstance.

SECTION 6: SHUTDOWN AND PRESERVATION

6.1 Following shutdown, turning gear must be engaged within 10 minutes and
operated continuously until rotor metal temperature falls below 150 degrees
Celsius.

6.2 For outages exceeding 30 days, the steam path must be preserved with
dehumidified air maintained below 40 percent relative humidity.
