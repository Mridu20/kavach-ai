SECTION 1: ROTOR & BEARING MAINTENANCE
1.1 Vibration Thresholds:
    - Normal operating vibration: < 1.5 mm/s RMS.
    - Warning threshold: 2.8 mm/s RMS — schedule rotor balancing during next maintenance cycle.
    - Critical Trip threshold: 4.5 mm/s RMS — automated turbine trip system engages within 3 seconds.

SECTION 2: LUBRICATION & COOLING
2.1 Synthetic Oil Specs: ISO VG 46 turbine oil.
2.2 Oil filter change interval: Every 2,000 operating hours or when differential pressure across filter exceeds 1.2 bar.

SECTION 3: SEAL & VALVE REPLACEMENT PROCEDURES
3.1 Secondary containment seal replacement: Required every 12 months for heavy steam turbine housing.
3.2 Main steam stop valve inspection: Perform dye penetrant testing on valve stem every 6 months.
