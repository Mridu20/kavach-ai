STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: INDUSTRIAL SAFETY AND PRESSURE VESSEL INSPECTION
Document ID: KAVACH-SOP-SAFETY-2025-V3
Revision: 3.0
Effective Date: 01 April 2025
Issuing Authority: Plant Safety and Integrity Department
Classification: INTERNAL - CONFIDENTIAL

SECTION 1: SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY

1.1 This procedure governs the inspection, testing and certification of all
pressure-retaining equipment operating above 1.0 bar gauge, including pressure
vessels, heat exchangers, steam drums, separators and associated piping.

1.2 This procedure applies to all refinery units, utility blocks and offsite
tankage under the operational control of the plant.

1.3 Personnel performing inspections under this procedure must hold a valid
Level II certification in the relevant non-destructive testing method.

SECTION 2: CORROSION THRESHOLDS AND MANDATORY SHUTDOWN PROTOCOL

2.1 Corrosion Tolerance Limit. The maximum allowable wall thickness
degradation for any pressure-retaining component is 0.5 mm measured against
the original certified thickness recorded in the equipment data book.

2.2 Where ultrasonic thickness measurement reveals wall thinning exceeding
0.5 mm, an immediate emergency shutdown of the affected equipment must be
initiated within 15 minutes of the measurement being confirmed by a second
qualified inspector.

2.3 Equipment shut down under clause 2.2 must not be returned to service until
a fitness-for-service assessment has been completed and countersigned by the
Head of Mechanical Integrity.

2.4 Corrosion rate is calculated as the difference between successive thickness
readings divided by the elapsed interval in years. A corrosion rate exceeding
0.1 mm per year requires the inspection interval to be halved.

SECTION 3: INSPECTION INTERVALS

3.1 Primary containment vessels: ultrasonic thickness survey every two months
at designated condition monitoring locations.

3.2 Secondary containment seals must be replaced every 12 months regardless of
apparent condition, or immediately upon any evidence of weeping or seepage.

3.3 Pressure relief valves must be removed, bench tested and recertified every
24 months. Set pressure drift beyond 3 percent of nameplate requires the valve
to be overhauled or replaced.

3.4 External visual inspection of insulated equipment must include removal of
insulation at a minimum of four inspection windows per vessel to check for
corrosion under insulation.

SECTION 4: WELD INSPECTION AND DEFECT ACCEPTANCE

4.1 All butt welds in pressure-retaining service require 100 percent
radiographic or ultrasonic examination prior to commissioning.

4.2 Surface breaking cracks of any length are unacceptable and require the weld
to be excavated and re-welded under an approved repair procedure.

4.3 Rounded indications up to 3 mm in maximum dimension are acceptable provided
no more than five such indications appear within any 150 mm length of weld.

4.4 Linear indications exceeding 6 mm require the weld to be rejected and the
affected section replaced.

SECTION 5: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT

5.1 Entry into any confined space requires a valid confined space entry permit,
continuous atmospheric monitoring and a dedicated standby attendant stationed
at the point of entry.

5.2 Minimum atmospheric requirements for entry are oxygen between 19.5 and 23.5
percent by volume, hydrogen sulphide below 10 parts per million, and lower
explosive limit below 10 percent.

5.3 Flame resistant coveralls, safety helmet, impact resistant eye protection
and steel toe footwear are mandatory in all process areas at all times.

SECTION 6: INCIDENT REPORTING

6.1 Any breach of the thresholds defined in Section 2 must be reported to the
Shift Superintendent immediately and logged in the plant incident register
within one hour.

6.2 A formal root cause analysis must be completed within 14 calendar days for
any incident involving loss of primary containment.
