STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: INDUSTRIAL SAFETY & PRESSURE VESSEL INSPECTION
Document ID: KAVACH-SOP-SAFETY-2025-V3
Classification: Sovereign Confidential Internal
================================================================================

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW & SCOPE
1.1 Purpose: To establish mandatory guidelines for inspection, pressure testing, and emergency shutdown of industrial boilers, high-pressure vessels, and fuel pipelines across facility units.
1.2 Scope: Mandatory for all inspection personnel, field engineers, and automated diagnostic agents.

SECTION 2: CORROSION THRESHOLDS & MANDATORY SHUTDOWN PROTOCOL
2.1 Corrosion Tolerance Limit:
    - Maximum allowable wall thickness degradation is 0.5 mm from initial baseline specs.
    - If non-destructive testing (NDT) reveals wall thinning exceeding 0.5 mm, an immediate emergency shutdown must be initiated within 15 minutes.
2.2 Inspection Intervals:
    - Primary containment vessels: Bi-monthly ultrasonic thickness check.
    - Secondary pressure relief valves: Quarterly recalibration and seal check.

SECTION 3: SEAL & VALVE REPLACEMENT PROCEDURES
3.1 Secondary containment seals: Must be replaced every 12 months, or immediately upon detection of micro-fractures during inspection.
3.2 Emergency Bypass Valves: Must undergo pneumatic actuation test every 30 days.

SECTION 4: HAZARD RESPONSE & CONTAINMENT
4.1 In the event of secondary pressure drop > 5% within 60 seconds:
    - Step 1: Engage automatic isolation damper.
    - Step 2: Trigger plant-wide alarm Level 2.
    - Step 3: Dispatch manual inspection team with safety gear.
